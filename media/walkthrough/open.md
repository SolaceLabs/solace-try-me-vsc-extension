# Open Solace Try Me

Select the Solace Try Me icon in the Activity Bar. The view has three sections:

- **Broker Config**: broker profiles and extension settings. Passwords are stored in your operating system's keychain.
- **Publish**: send messages to topics or queues, or send a request and wait for the reply.
- **Subscribe**: subscribe to topics, consume or browse queues, and inspect received messages.

Use the "Open in a New Tab" button in the view title to open more Try Me tabs.
